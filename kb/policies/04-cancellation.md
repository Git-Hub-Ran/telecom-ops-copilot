# Cancellation

How to cancel TelSano service, what fees apply, and what happens after cancellation.

## No long term contracts

TelSano does not require long term contracts on any plan. Customers can cancel at any time without an early termination fee.

This applies to all plans: Essential, Connect, Unlimited, Internet 100, and Fiber 1000.

## How to cancel

A customer can cancel in two ways:

1. In the customer portal: telsano.example.com/account > "Manage Services" > "Cancel"
2. By contacting support and confirming the cancellation request

Cancellation through the mobile app is not currently supported. The customer must use the portal or talk to support.

## When cancellation takes effect

Cancellation takes effect **at the end of the current billing cycle**, not immediately. This means the customer keeps their service until the cycle ends, even after requesting cancellation.

Example: a customer with a billing cycle from the 5th to the 4th of next month requests cancellation on the 15th. Service continues until the 4th of next month. After that, the line or internet connection is disabled.

The customer is not billed for the next cycle.

## Prorated refunds

If a customer paid in advance for the current cycle and requests cancellation, no refund is given for the days remaining in that cycle. They keep the service through the end of the cycle they already paid for.

The only exception is if the customer is canceling due to a TelSano error (wrong service activated, service never worked). In that case, a prorated refund is issued.

## Equipment return

Customers who rented or received equipment from TelSano must return it within **14 days** of the cancellation effective date.

Equipment includes:

- Wi-Fi 6 router (Fiber 1000 customers)
- Cable modem (Internet 100 customers who rented from TelSano)
- Any TelSano provided mobile device under a payment plan

A return shipping label is sent by email when cancellation is confirmed. Drop off is available at any UPS location at no cost to the customer.

## Unreturned equipment fees

If equipment is not returned within 14 days, the following fees apply:

| Equipment | Fee |
|---|---|
| Wi-Fi 6 router | $100 |
| Cable modem | $50 |
| Mobile device (varies) | Remaining balance on payment plan |

The fee is charged to the payment method on file. If no payment method is available, the customer receives an invoice and a 30 day window to pay before the account goes to collections.

## Number portability

If the customer wants to keep their mobile number when canceling, they need to start a number transfer (called "porting") with their new carrier **before** TelSano disables the line. Once the line is disabled, the number is released and may not be recoverable.

The customer can ask support for their account number and PIN, which the new carrier will need to complete the transfer.

## Reactivation

If a customer cancels and wants to reactivate within **30 days**, the previous plan and any saved settings can usually be restored. There is no reactivation fee in this window.

After 30 days, the customer must sign up as a new customer. Previous account number and any saved data are deleted.

## Cancellation due to moving

Customers who are moving and want to keep their service should contact support **before** moving rather than canceling. TelSano can transfer many addresses without a service interruption. Fiber 1000 may not be available at the new address, in which case the customer can downgrade to Internet 100 or cancel without penalty.
