# Billing Cycle

How TelSano bills its customers, when bills are issued, and when payment is due.

## When bills are issued

Each customer has a personal billing cycle that starts on the day they signed up. The bill is issued on the first day of every cycle and covers the upcoming month of service.

For example: a customer who signed up on the 5th of the month will receive a bill on the 5th of every following month.

## When payment is due

Payment is due **21 days after the bill issue date**.

For example: if your bill is issued on the 5th of the month, payment is due on the 26th of that month.

## What is included on the bill

Each bill includes:

- Monthly plan charges for all active services
- One-time charges (activation, equipment purchases, technician visits)
- Taxes and regulatory fees (varies by state)
- Discounts applied (autopay, bundle, family, senior, military)
- Account credits from previous adjustments

## Prorated charges

When a customer changes plans or adds a line in the middle of a billing cycle, the bill is prorated. This means the customer pays only for the days the new service was active in that cycle.

Example: a customer on the Essential plan ($25 per month) upgrades to Unlimited ($65 per month) on day 15 of a 30 day cycle. The bill for that cycle includes:

- 14 days on Essential: about $11.67
- 16 days on Unlimited: about $34.67
- Total for that month: about $46.34

The full Unlimited rate ($65) applies to the next full cycle.

## Where to view your bill

Customers can view current and past bills in three places:

- The TelSano mobile app, under "Account" then "Billing"
- The customer portal at telsano.example.com/account
- A monthly email summary sent to the email on file

## Paper bills

Customers who prefer paper bills delivered by mail can request them by contacting support. There is a $5 monthly fee for paper billing. Most customers find the app or email versions easier to use.

## Bill questions

If a charge looks wrong, customers should contact support within 60 days of the bill date. After 60 days, billing disputes are reviewed case by case and may not be reversed.
