# Refunds and Account Credits

When TelSano gives money back, when it gives an account credit, and how to request either.

## Refund vs credit

There are two ways TelSano returns money:

- **Refund**: the money goes back to the original payment method (card or bank account). Typically used for one-time errors or returns.
- **Account credit**: the money is applied to the next bill, reducing what the customer owes. Typically used for service outages or small adjustments.

Customers can usually choose which one they prefer, except in cases noted below.

## When a refund or credit applies

### Service outages

If a service is fully unavailable for **more than 24 hours** and the outage was on TelSano's side (not the customer's equipment), the customer is eligible for a credit.

The credit is calculated as: (monthly plan price) divided by (days in the month) times (days of outage).

Example: a Fiber 1000 customer ($80 per month) loses service for 3 days in a 30 day month. The credit is: $80 / 30 * 3 = $8.00

Credits for outages are usually applied automatically to the next bill within 7 days of the outage being resolved. If the customer does not see the credit, they should contact support.

### Billing errors

If a charge on the bill is wrong (duplicate charge, charge for a service the customer does not have, incorrect promotion not applied), the customer can request a correction.

Corrections are issued as refunds to the original payment method when the customer has already paid. If the customer has not paid yet, the bill is adjusted before the due date.

Billing error requests must be made within **60 days** of the bill date.

### Equipment returns

When a customer returns a device or accessory bought from TelSano within **30 days** of purchase, they get a full refund minus any restocking fee.

Restocking fees:

- Sealed and unopened: no fee
- Opened but in good condition: $25
- Opened with damage or missing parts: refund denied, customer may keep the item

Refunds for returns are processed within 7 to 10 business days after the item is received at the TelSano warehouse.

### Goodwill credits

Support agents can issue **goodwill credits** to apologize for poor service experiences (long wait times, repeated issues, mishandled support tickets). These credits are usually $5 to $25 and are applied to the next bill.

Goodwill credits are not promised in writing and are at the agent's discretion. Each account can receive up to $50 in goodwill credits per year without manager approval.

## How to request a refund or credit

The customer can request a refund or credit in three ways:

1. Through the support chat in the TelSano mobile app
2. Through the customer portal: "Help" > "Billing Issues"
3. By calling support

For credits under $25, support agents can usually approve immediately. Larger amounts may need supervisor review and take 24 to 48 hours to be approved.

## Processing time

| Type | Time |
|---|---|
| Account credit | Visible on next bill, applied within 24 hours of approval |
| Refund to credit card | 5 to 7 business days |
| Refund to bank account (ACH) | 7 to 10 business days |
| Refund by mailed check (rare) | 14 to 21 business days |

## What is not eligible for refund

- Taxes and regulatory fees collected by TelSano on behalf of governments
- Activation fees once the service has been used
- Plan charges for service the customer used (no buyer's remorse refunds)
- Charges older than 60 days that were not previously disputed
