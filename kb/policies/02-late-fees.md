# Late Fees and Late Payment

What happens when a TelSano customer pays after the due date.

## Grace period

TelSano offers a **5 day grace period** after the bill due date. Payments received during this window are not considered late and do not trigger any fee.

Example: if a bill is due on the 26th of the month, payment is still on time if it arrives by the 31st (or the 1st of the next month for shorter months).

## Late fee amount

If payment has not arrived after the 5 day grace period, a **$10 late fee** is added to the next bill.

The late fee is charged once per missed payment. Customers who pay late multiple times in a year may be contacted about setting up autopay.

## Service suspension

If a bill is **14 days past due** (counting from the original due date, not the grace period end), TelSano may suspend service. Suspension means:

- Mobile lines: no calls, texts, or data. Emergency calls (911) still work.
- Home internet: connection disabled.

The customer is notified by email and text 3 days before any suspension. Suspension does not happen automatically. A support agent reviews the account first.

## Reconnection fee

To restore service after suspension, the customer must:

1. Pay the full past due amount
2. Pay a **$25 reconnection fee** per suspended line or service

Reconnection usually takes 15 to 30 minutes after payment is processed.

## Disputing a late fee

A customer may dispute a late fee in any of these cases:

- The bill was not received (email bounced, mail delivery issue)
- A payment was made on time but processed late by the bank
- There is a billing error on the original charge

To dispute, the customer contacts support within 30 days of the late fee charge. The agent can waive the fee one time per account in any 12 month period at their discretion. Repeated waivers require manager approval.

## Credit reporting

TelSano does not report standard late payments to credit bureaus. However, accounts that are **more than 90 days past due** and have been sent to collections are reported, which can affect the customer's credit score.

## Avoiding late fees

The easiest way to avoid late fees is to enroll in autopay. Autopay customers also get a $5 per month discount on each mobile line. See the autopay policy document for details.
