# Autopay

How automatic monthly billing works at TelSano and how to set it up, change it, or turn it off.

## What autopay does

When autopay is enabled, TelSano automatically charges the customer's saved payment method on the bill due date each month. The customer does not need to log in or take any action.

The customer always receives a bill email 5 days before the charge, so they know what to expect.

## The autopay discount

Customers on autopay receive **$5 off per mobile line, every month**.

The discount applies to each line on the account, not just the first. The discount does not apply to home internet plans.

Example: a family with three Connect mobile lines on autopay saves $15 per month ($5 times 3 lines).

The discount appears as a separate line item on the bill, labeled "Autopay discount."

## Accepted payment methods

- Credit cards: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover
- Debit cards (with a Visa or Mastercard logo)
- Bank account (ACH transfer) through a US bank

Prepaid cards and international cards are not supported.

## How to enroll

A customer can sign up for autopay in three ways:

1. In the TelSano mobile app: "Account" > "Billing" > "Set up Autopay"
2. In the customer portal: telsano.example.com/account > "Payment"
3. By contacting support and providing payment information securely

Enrollment takes effect on the next billing cycle, not the current one.

## What happens if a payment fails

Sometimes a payment does not go through (expired card, insufficient funds, bank issue). When this happens:

- The customer is notified immediately by email and text
- TelSano automatically retries the payment **3 days later**
- If the second attempt also fails, autopay is paused
- The customer is moved to manual payment until they fix the payment method

A failed autopay does not trigger an instant late fee. The customer still has the regular 5 day grace period to update their payment method and pay the bill.

## Changing the payment method on autopay

The customer can change the saved payment method at any time without disabling autopay. The new method is used starting from the next scheduled charge.

If a card is updated within 7 days of the next charge, that charge may still use the old card. Setting a new method 8 or more days before the due date guarantees the new method is used.

## Turning off autopay

Autopay can be turned off at any time in the app or portal. There is no fee.

When autopay is turned off:

- The $5 per line discount is removed starting the next billing cycle
- The customer must pay each bill manually by the due date
- All existing protections (grace period, late fee, etc.) apply normally

## Refunds when overcharged

If autopay charges the wrong amount (rare, but possible), the customer should contact support. Refunds are processed back to the same payment method and typically appear within 3 to 5 business days.
