# Essential Plan

Our entry-level mobile plan, designed for customers who use their phone mostly for messaging, maps, and light browsing.

## What's included

- 5 GB of high-speed data per month
- Unlimited talk and text within the United States
- Free calls between TelSano mobile lines
- Visual voicemail

## What's not included

- Mobile hotspot (tethering to other devices)
- International calling
- International roaming
- HD video streaming (streaming is capped at 480p)

## What happens after 5 GB

Once you use 5 GB of high-speed data in a billing cycle, your speed is reduced to 128 kbps for the rest of the month. You can still send messages and use light apps, but streaming and large downloads will be slow. Your full speed returns on the first day of the next billing cycle.

## Fair use policy

Speeds may be reduced during network congestion after 22 GB of total monthly usage. This is rare for Essential customers because the high-speed cap is only 5 GB.

## Who it's for

The Essential plan is a good fit for:

- Customers who mainly use messaging apps and maps
- Backup or secondary lines
- Light data users on a budget
- Children's first phone with parental control

## How to upgrade

You can switch to the Connect or Unlimited plan at any time. The new monthly price takes effect on your next billing cycle. No upgrade fees apply.
