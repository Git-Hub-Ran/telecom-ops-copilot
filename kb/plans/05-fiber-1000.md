# Fiber 1000 Plan

Our top home internet plan. Symmetric fiber speeds for heavy streaming, gaming, content creation, and large households.

## What's included

- 1 Gbps download speed (1000 Mbps)
- 1 Gbps upload speed (symmetric)
- No monthly data cap
- Wi-Fi 6 router included at no extra cost
- Professional installation included at no extra cost
- 24/7 priority phone and chat support
- Free network security suite (firewall, parental controls, anti-malware)

## What's not included

- 10 Gbps multi-gig speeds (Fiber 1000 caps at 1 Gbps)
- Static IP address (available as an add-on for $5/month)
- TV service (we are an internet-only provider)

## One-time charges

- No activation fee
- No installation fee
- No equipment fee (the router stays with TelSano and must be returned if you cancel)

## What makes fiber different

Fiber internet uses light through glass cables instead of copper wires. The benefits:

- Upload speed is the same as download (good for video calls, cloud backups, content uploads)
- Latency is very low (good for gaming and live video)
- The connection is more stable, especially during peak hours

## Typical use

1 Gbps is enough for:

- Multiple 4K streams at the same time
- Competitive online gaming
- Live streaming and video uploading
- Large household with many smart devices
- Working from home with frequent large file uploads

## Installation timeline

A technician visit is required. Most customers are scheduled within 5-7 business days of ordering. The visit takes 1-3 hours depending on whether fiber is already wired to your home.

## Who it's for

The Fiber 1000 plan is a good fit for:

- Heavy streamers (multiple 4K screens at once)
- Online gamers who want low latency
- Content creators who upload often
- Households with 4 or more people
- Remote workers who upload large files

## How to change plans

You can move down to Internet 100 at any time. You will need to return the Wi-Fi 6 router within 14 days to avoid a $100 unreturned-equipment fee.
