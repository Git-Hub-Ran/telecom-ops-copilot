# Connect Plan

Our mid-tier mobile plan, built for moderate data users who want hotspot and a bit of international access.

## What's included

- 25 GB of high-speed data per month
- 5 GB of mobile hotspot per month
- Unlimited talk and text within the United States, Canada, and Mexico
- 100 minutes of international calling per month to 50 countries
- Standard streaming quality (480p video)
- Visual voicemail and Wi-Fi calling

## What's not included

- HD video streaming above 480p
- Hotspot above 5 GB at full speed
- International data roaming
- Calls to countries outside the 50-country list at no extra charge

## What happens after 25 GB

Once you use 25 GB of high-speed data in a billing cycle, your speed is reduced to 600 kbps for the rest of the month. This is still fast enough for messaging, email, music streaming, and standard video. Speeds reset on the first day of the next billing cycle.

After your 5 GB of hotspot is used, hotspot speeds drop to 600 kbps as well.

## Fair use policy

During times of heavy network congestion, customers who have used more than 50 GB in a month may see slower speeds in busy areas. This usually only affects users near the top of the cap.

## Who it's for

The Connect plan is a good fit for:

- Customers with moderate data needs (streaming, social media, video calls)
- People who occasionally need hotspot for laptop work
- Families with one or two devices
- Customers who call relatives in Canada, Mexico, or one of the 50 supported countries

## How to upgrade or downgrade

You can move up to Unlimited or down to Essential at any time. The change takes effect on your next billing cycle.
