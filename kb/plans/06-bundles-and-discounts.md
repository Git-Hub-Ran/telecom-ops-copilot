# Bundles and Discounts

TelSano offers several ways to lower your monthly bill when you combine plans or qualify for a discount program.

## Mobile + Home Internet bundle

Combine any mobile plan with any home internet plan and save $15 per month on your total bill.

The discount applies automatically once both services are active on the same account. There is no separate sign-up.

### Example: Connect + Internet 100

- Connect mobile: $45/month
- Internet 100 home: $50/month
- Subtotal: $95/month
- Bundle discount: -$15/month
- **Final monthly price: $80**

### Example: Unlimited + Fiber 1000

- Unlimited mobile: $65/month
- Fiber 1000 home: $80/month
- Subtotal: $145/month
- Bundle discount: -$15/month
- **Final monthly price: $130**

## Autopay discount

Sign up for autopay (automatic monthly billing to a card or bank account) and get $5 off per mobile line, every month.

The discount applies to each line on the account, not just the first one. It does not apply to home internet plans.

### Example with autopay

A customer with one Unlimited line on autopay:

- Unlimited: $65/month
- Autopay discount: -$5/month
- **Final: $60/month**

A family with three Connect lines on autopay:

- 3 × Connect: $135/month
- 3 × Autopay discount: -$15/month
- **Final: $120/month**

## Family discounts (multi-line)

When you have multiple mobile lines on one account, additional lines get a discount:

- 1st line: full price
- 2nd line: $10 off
- 3rd line: $15 off
- 4th line and beyond: $20 off each

### Example: 3 Unlimited lines

- 1st Unlimited: $65
- 2nd Unlimited: $65 - $10 = $55
- 3rd Unlimited: $65 - $15 = $50
- **Total: $170/month** (before autopay)

## Senior discount

Customers aged 55 or older get $10 off any mobile plan, every month. You need to provide proof of age when signing up (driver's license or passport). The discount stays with the line even if you change plans later.

The senior discount cannot be combined with the military discount on the same line.

## Military and first responder discount

Active military, veterans, first responders (police, fire, EMT), and nurses get 15% off any mobile plan. Eligibility is verified through ID.me at sign-up and is rechecked once a year.

The military discount cannot be combined with the senior discount on the same line.

## Stacking the discounts

Most discounts can be combined. For example, a senior customer with autopay on a bundle:

- Connect mobile: $45
- Senior discount: -$10
- Autopay discount: -$5
- Internet 100: $50
- Bundle discount: -$15
- **Final monthly bill: $65**

The exceptions are the senior and military discounts, which cannot be applied to the same line at the same time.

## Discounts that do NOT apply

- Discounts do not apply to one-time charges like activation or equipment fees.
- Discounts do not apply to roaming or international calling overages.
- Discounts cannot be applied retroactively to past bills.

## How to add a discount

Customers can add autopay, family, senior, or military discounts through the TelSano app, the customer portal, or by contacting support. Most discounts apply on the next billing cycle, not the current one.
