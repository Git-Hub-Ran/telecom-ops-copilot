# Unlimited Plan

Our top-tier mobile plan for heavy users, frequent travelers, and customers who do not want to worry about data caps.

## What's included

- Unlimited high-speed data (no monthly cap)
- 25 GB of mobile hotspot per month
- Unlimited talk and text within the United States, Canada, and Mexico
- Unlimited international texts to over 200 countries
- HD video streaming (up to 1080p)
- Free in-flight Wi-Fi on selected airlines (one hour per flight)
- Visual voicemail, Wi-Fi calling, and 5G access where available

## What's not included

- 4K UHD video streaming (streaming is capped at 1080p)
- International calling minutes (texts are free, calls are billed per minute)
- Hotspot above 25 GB at full speed

## How hotspot works

You get 25 GB of high-speed hotspot per month. After 25 GB, hotspot speeds drop to 600 kbps for the rest of the cycle. Your phone's own data remains unlimited at high speed.

## Fair use policy

During times of heavy network congestion, customers who have used more than 50 GB in a month may see temporarily slower speeds in busy areas. The data itself remains unlimited; only the priority changes briefly. Speeds return to normal once congestion clears.

## Who it's for

The Unlimited plan is a good fit for:

- Heavy data users (daily streaming, large downloads, video calls)
- Frequent travelers within North America
- Customers who use their phone as a primary internet device
- People who often work from a hotspot

## How to change plans

You can move down to Connect or Essential at any time. The change takes effect on your next billing cycle. No fees apply.
