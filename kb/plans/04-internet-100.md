# Internet 100 Plan

Our standard home internet plan. Reliable speed for everyday browsing, streaming, and remote work in a small household.

## What's included

- 100 Mbps download speed
- 20 Mbps upload speed
- No monthly data cap (truly unlimited)
- 24/7 chat support
- Self-install kit shipped to your address
- Network security basics (firewall, parental controls)

## What's not included

- Router or modem (you bring your own, or you can buy one from TelSano for $80 as a one-time charge)
- Professional installation (self-install only on this plan)
- Static IP address (available as an add-on for $5/month)

## One-time charges

- Activation fee: $10 (one-time, charged on your first bill)
- Equipment fee if you order from TelSano: $80 (one-time)

## Typical use

100 Mbps is enough for:

- 2-3 people streaming HD video at the same time
- Video calls for remote work
- Online gaming for one or two players
- Smart home devices

It may feel slow if you have several people streaming 4K at once or large frequent file uploads (in that case, Fiber 1000 is a better fit).

## Installation timeline

Most customers get their kit within 2 business days and complete setup in under 30 minutes. If you have trouble with self-install, chat support can guide you, or you can request a paid technician visit ($60).

## Who it's for

The Internet 100 plan is a good fit for:

- Small households (1-3 people)
- Light to moderate streaming
- Remote workers with standard video call needs
- Customers comfortable plugging in their own router

## How to upgrade

You can switch to Fiber 1000 at any time. There is no upgrade fee, but a technician visit is needed to install the fiber line. The new monthly price starts on the day your fiber line is activated.
