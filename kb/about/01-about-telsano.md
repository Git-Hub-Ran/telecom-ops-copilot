# About TelSano

TelSano is a US telecom provider serving residential customers across the
United States.

## Mobile plans

TelSano offers three mobile plans: Essential (basic talk, text, and data),
Connect (mid-tier with increased data and hotspot), and Unlimited (unlimited
data with priority network access).

## Home internet plans

TelSano offers two home internet plans: Internet 100 (100 Mbps, cable-based)
and Fiber 1000 (1 Gbps, fiber optic).

## Support

Customers can manage their account, view billing history, and get technical
support through TelSano's customer service channel.
